## About Us | 关于我们

We are **FedOceanLab**, a research team from **Dalian Ocean University**, China. Our research focuses on **federated learning** and **trustworthy data circulation**, exploring how multiple parties can collaborate on data and models while protecting privacy and maintaining trust.

我们是来自**大连海洋大学**的 **FedOceanLab**。团队专注于**联邦学习、数据可信流转**等方向，探索在保护数据隐私、建立可信协作机制的基础上，实现跨主体的数据利用与模型协同，让数据价值在可信协作中得到释放。

## Research Interests | 研究方向

- **Federated Learning | 联邦学习**  
  Collaborative model training across distributed data sources, with an emphasis on privacy, heterogeneity, and reliability.  
  面向分布式数据的协同建模，关注隐私保护、数据异构与协作可靠性。

- **Trustworthy Data Circulation | 数据可信流转**  
  Methods and mechanisms for secure, controllable, and traceable data sharing and use across organizations.  
  探索跨主体数据共享与利用中的安全保障、使用可控与过程可追溯机制。

- **Privacy-Preserving Collaboration | 隐私保护协作**  
  Approaches that balance data utility, privacy protection, and trust in collaborative learning.  
  探索兼顾数据效用、隐私保护与协作可信性的技术方法。

## Our Vision | 我们的愿景

To enable trustworthy collaboration that connects distributed data and turns shared knowledge into collective intelligence.

以可信协作连接分散的数据，让知识共享推动群体智能的发展。

欢迎对联邦学习、数据可信流转及相关领域感兴趣的研究者、同学与开发者交流合作。欢迎浏览我们的项目，并通过相关仓库的 Issues 与我们交流。
